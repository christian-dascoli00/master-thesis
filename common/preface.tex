\chapter*{Preface}
\fontsize{12}{14.5}\selectfont

In this thesis I explore the notion of ideally exact category, introduced by George Janelidze in 2024. The main goal is to provide an overview of the ideally exact categories with the necessary background needed to understand it, and present some of the most important results in this field.

A basic knowledge of category theory, such as limits, colimits, adjunctions, and monads is assumed throughout this thesis. Some familiarity with Malt'sev, exact, regular, protomodular and semiabelian categories would provide a natural foundation; however the main constructions and definitions of these categories are reviewed in the first chapter. The reader who find it helpful can refer to the appendix, where some results about these categories are presented.

Right after the preliminaries, the first chapter introduces the motivating example of this theory, which is the category of rings with unit. This example brings us to ideally exact categories, which capture the essential categorical features of ideals and rings.

The second chapter presents the pullback functor and its left adjoint, which are a key tool in the theory of ideally exact categories. In this chapter I also define the connected limits, since they have useful properties with the mentioned adjunction. Then, I will introduce the notion of nullary and essentially nullary monads and their results, which will be used the prove the main result of the ideally exact categories.

The third chapter is devoted to some results concerning the lifting of properties to the Eilenberg-Moore category of a monad. This part is quite technical and articulate. It will be useful to characterize the notion of ideal exact category.

The fourth chapter is dedicated to the study a monadicity theorem for the pullback functor in an exact category.

The fifth deals with an overview of the notion of ideal exact category, which is the main topic of this thesis. I will present the definition and the characterization of this notion, and some of its properties. Then, I will show the main theorem. This result has been central in motivating interest in this topic.

In the sixth chapter I recall the notion of topos and its dual, a cotopos. Then, I will show that this last is an example of ideally exact category. Finally, I will analyze the more concrete example of the category of dual of sets and try to understand how the ideals work in this case.
