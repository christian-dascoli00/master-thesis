\section{The pullback functor}

In this section we will define the pullback functor and its left adjoint, the shrink functor.

Given a category $\mathcal{A}$ with pullbacks and a morphism $p: E \to A$ in $\mathcal{A}$, one can consider adjunction

\[
\begin{tikzcd}
    (\mathcal{A} \downarrow B)
  \arrow[bend left=40]{r}[description]{p^*}
  & (\mathcal{A} \downarrow E)
  \arrow[bend left=40]{l}[description]{p_!}
  \arrow[phantom, shift left=1.5, shift right=1.5]{l}[description]{\rotatebox{180}{$\top$}}
\end{tikzcd}
\]

\noindent defined as follows:

\begin{itemize}
    \item $(\mathcal{A} \downarrow B)$ is the category of objects over $B$ in $\mathcal{A}$, we denote \\ an object by $(A, \alpha:A \to B)$;
    \item $(\mathcal{A} \downarrow E)$ is the category of objects over $E$ in $\mathcal{A}$, we denote \\ an object by $(D, \delta:D \to E)$;
    \item $p^*$ is the pullback functor;
    \item $p_!$ is the shrink functor.
\end{itemize}

The pullback and shrink functor are defined as follows:

\[
    p^*: (\mathcal{A} \downarrow E) \to (\mathcal{A} \downarrow B)
\]
